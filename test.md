# Griselda's Path: Siege of Metabolism

## The Setup
Griselda, a modern, high-performance athlete, pushes herself so hard in her latest HIIT session that she unlocks a metaphysical 'state of flow' so intense it becomes a physical world. She materializes in **The Path of Performance**, a realm where her own athletic discipline and kinetic energy are the ground she walks on and the sky she sees. The existing environment is a projection of her focus.

## The Problem
This state of being is under attack by **The Great Inertia**. This force manifests as **Languor Wraiths** (the red ghosts), the literal embodiments of skipped workouts and deferred effort. They don't want to destroy her; they want to put her in a state of perpetual rest, slowing her kinetic drive to a halt. When she slows down, the entire world begins to calcify and solidify into a state of heavy, permanent rest.

## The Quest
Armed with her 'Sword of Focus' and 'Shield of Dedication' (her re-contextualized gear), Griselda must sprint across surreal landscapes that project her own goals (e.g., stairs leading up). The Wraiths are now actively trying to make her *stop moving*. Every time she stops, her glowing **Metabolism Meter** (visible on her wrist and the UI) is attacked, draining her kinetic energy. She must defeat the wrappers to regain focus and accelerate.

## The Weird Goal
The goal isn't just an escape; it's a colossal, **Golden Treadmill** that acts as the world's power engine. If she can reach the Treadmill, set it in motion, and maintain a certain speed for a set duration, she creates a critical burst of kinetic force that banishes The Great Inertia and sends her back to the real world fully rejuvenated, having 'beaten' laziness itself.
