# SN74HC245 -- Octal bus transceiver

**PDF:** [`../SN74HC245_bus_transceiver.pdf`](../SN74HC245_bus_transceiver.pdf).  
**Package:** 20-pin PDIP.  
**Qty (v0):** **3** -- CPU bus / OAM path / cart isolation.  
**Qty (v1 proposal):** **1** -- CPU vs video data path; other isolation via PLD `/OE`.  
**Typical `tpd`:** ~12 ns @ 5 V. 3-state outputs.

## What it is

Bidirectional **8-bit** transceiver. **DIR** chooses A->B or B->A. **OE** (active low) enables drivers; OE high = both sides Hi-Z (buses isolated).

## Retr01 role

Keep **one driver** on shared data nets: isolate CPU D[7:0] from cart, OAM capture, or video-side peek paths when those domains own the bus.

## 20-pin PDIP

```text
         +-----\/-----+
    DIR  | 1       20 | VCC
     A1  | 2       19 | OE
     A2  | 3       18 | B1
     A3  | 4       17 | B2
     A4  | 5       16 | B3
     A5  | 6       15 | B4
     A6  | 7       14 | B5
     A7  | 8       13 | B6
     A8  | 9       12 | B7
    GND  |10       11 | B8
         +------------+
```

## Function

| OE | DIR | Action |
|----|-----|--------|
| H | X | A and B **Hi-Z** |
| L | H | **A -> B** (A inputs, B outputs) |
| L | L | **B -> A** (B inputs, A outputs) |

## Expected I/O

| Stimulus | Expect |
|----------|--------|
| OE=L, DIR=H, A=`$55` | B=`$55` |
| OE=H | Neither side drives |
| Two HC245s on one net both OE=L | **Bus fight** -- sim must flag |

## Communication

```text
CPU D[7:0] --A-- HC245 --B-- cart / peripheral / video peek
                 ^
            DIR, OE from decode PLD
```
