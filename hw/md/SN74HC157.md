# SN74HC157 -- Quad 2:1 mux

**PDF:** [`../SN74HC157_mux.pdf`](../SN74HC157_mux.pdf).  
**Package:** 16-pin PDIP.  
**Qty (current):** **6** -- VRAM address mux **x3** + line-buffer address mux **x3** ([`06`](../../docs/06_hardware_v1_32ic.md)).  
**Qty (legacy sketches):** some notes used 4+2; normative split is **3+3**.  
**Logic family:** 74HC, 2-6 V (Retr01: 5 V). Typical `tpd` ~11 ns @ 5 V.

## What it is

Four independent **2-line to 1-line** selectors sharing one **A/B** select and one active-low **strobe G**. When G is high, all Y outputs are forced **low** (not Hi-Z).

## Retr01 role

| Group | Selects between |
|-------|-----------------|
| 3x chips | CPU VRAM address vs BG-fetch address (interleave) |
| 3x chips | ATmega1284P line-buffer address vs beam read address |

Each chip muxes 4 address bits; three chips cover 12 bits (extend with PLD/glue for remaining address bits as needed).

## 16-pin PDIP

```text
         +-----\/-----+
    A/B  | 1       16 | VCC
     1A  | 2       15 | G
     1B  | 3       14 | 4A
     1Y  | 4       13 | 4B
     2A  | 5       12 | 4Y
     2B  | 6       11 | 3A
     2Y  | 7       10 | 3B
    GND  | 8        9 | 3Y
         +------------+
```

G = pin 15 (active-low strobe). A/B = pin 1.

## Function

| G | A/B | Yn |
|---|-----|----|
| H | X | **L** (forced) |
| L | L | **nA** |
| L | H | **nB** |

## Expected I/O

| Stimulus | Expect |
|----------|--------|
| G=L, A/B=L | Y copies A bus |
| G=L, A/B=H | Y copies B bus |
| G=H | All Y = 0 |

## Communication

```text
CPU addr ----A----\ 
                   HC157 --Y--> AS6C62256 VRAM A[]
Beam addr ----B----/   ^
                       A/B, G from PLD / PHI2 phase
```

Same pattern for line-buffer mux (1284 vs beam).
