## What it is

Synchronous presettable **4-bit binary** counter with:

- **CLK** (rising edge)
- **CLR** async clear (active low) -- forces QA-QD low
- **LOAD** sync parallel load (active low)
- **ENP**, **ENT** count enables (both high to count)
- **RCO** ripple carry out (high at count 15 when ENT high) for cascading

## Retr01 role

Dot-clocked beam:

| Pair | Range | Use |
|------|-------|-----|
| HC161 x2 | X = 0..340 | HBlank / active / wrap |
| HC161 x2 | Y = 0..261 | VBlank / NMI / raster |

Wrap and terminal-count decode use glue or PLD on RCO / bit patterns (341x262 timing from `02`/`03`).

## 16-pin PDIP

```text
         +-----\/-----+
    CLR  | 1       16 | VCC
    CLK  | 2       15 | RCO
      A  | 3       14 | QA
      B  | 4       13 | QB
      C  | 5       12 | QC
      D  | 6       11 | QD
    ENP  | 7       10 | ENT
    GND  | 8        9 | LOAD
         +------------+
```

QA = LSB, QD = MSB of the nibble. A-D = parallel load data.

## Function (sync, after CLK edge)

| CLR | LOAD | ENP | ENT | Action |
|-----|------|-----|-----|--------|
| L | X | X | X | Async clear (immediate) |
| H | L | X | X | Load A-D into QA-QD |
| H | H | H | H | Count +1 |
| H | H | else | | Hold |

RCO = ENT & (QA&QB&QC&QD) -- look-ahead for next stage.

## Expected I/O

| Stimulus | Expect |
|----------|--------|
| CLR pulse | Outputs 0 |
| LOAD with D=5, then count | 5,6,7,... |
| Cascade: low RCO -> high ENT | 8-bit / 9-bit chain counts in lockstep |
| Free-run at dot clock | Period matches programmed wrap logic |

## Communication

```text
dot CLK --> CLK
PLD wrap / LOAD / CLR --> controls
QA-QD, RCO --> next HC161 / HC688 / video timing
```
