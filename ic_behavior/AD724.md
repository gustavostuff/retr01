# AD724 behavior

Analog Devices **AD724** RGB to NTSC/PAL encoder. Retr01 qty **1** on the motherboard (counted in the **17**).

**Package:** native **SOIC-16** only. v1 DIY board mounts it on a **SOIC-16 to DIP-16 adapter** into a DIP-16 socket so the main PCB stays 100% THT.

**Board:** `../general_docs/hardware.md`. RGB analog always comes from the Color PROM DAC. Composite is AD724 -> J9 RCA.

---

## 1. Inputs (what pins can sense)

| Pin | Name | Dir | What the net must provide |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **STND** | IN | H = NTSC encode. L = PAL encode |
| 2 | **AGND** | PWR | Analog ground |
| 3 | **FIN** | IN | FSC clock, parallel-resonant crystal, or 4FSC clock |
| 4 | **APOS** | PWR | Analog +5 V |
| 5 | **ENCD** | IN | H = encode on. L = power down |
| 6 | **RIN** | IN | Red 0..714 mV AC-coupled |
| 7 | **GIN** | IN | Green 0..714 mV AC-coupled |
| 8 | **BIN** | IN | Blue 0..714 mV AC-coupled |
| 12 | **SELECT** | IN | L = FSC mode. H = 4FSC mode |
| 13 | **DGND** | PWR | Digital ground |
| 14 | **DPOS** | PWR | Digital +5 V |
| 15 | **VSYNC** | IN | Vertical sync, or hold > +2 V when feeding CSYNC on HSYNC |
| 16 | **HSYNC** | IN | Horizontal sync, **or** CSYNC when VSYNC is held high |

### Retr01 sync modes (J2)

| Mode | HSYNC pin | VSYNC pin |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **CSYNC** | Composite sync from Beam PLDs | Tied inactive / >2 V per datasheet CSYNC recipe |
| **H/V** | HSYNC | VSYNC |

Same J2 connector. Solder jumper or 1x3 header chooses the story. Do not drive both meanings onto pin 4 of J2 at once.

### Clock (Y3)

| Standard | FSC | 4FSC |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NTSC | 3.579545 MHz | 14.318180 MHz |
| PAL | 4.433618 MHz | 17.734480 MHz |

Retr01 baseline: FSC crystal into FIN with SELECT low. AD725 stays off the BOM.

### Stimulus checklist

| Stimulus | Expect |
| --- | --- |
| ENCD=H, valid RGB + sync + FSC | COMP / LUMA / CRMA present |
| ENCD=L | Outputs powered down |
| STND high vs low | NTSC vs PAL chroma rules |
| CSYNC on HSYNC with VSYNC held high | Composite sync accepted |

---

## 2. Process (what happens inside)

Fixed analog + logic encoder. Converts R/G/B into luminance and chrominance per NTSC or PAL, and combines them into composite. On-chip filters and delay line keep Y/C time-aligned. Drives 75 ohm reverse-terminated loads. No external delay lines required.

Logical inputs are CMOS/TTL compatible. Analog RGB expects ~0.7 Vpp video after the PROM DAC network.

Not programmable. No firmware. Not flashed through Adafruit's UPDI Friend.

---

## 3. Outputs (what pins can drive)

| Pin | Name | Dir | What the chip drives |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 9 | **CRMA** | OUT | Chrominance (~1.8 Vpp typ). S-video C later if wanted |
| 10 | **COMP** | OUT | Composite video (~2.5 Vpp typ) -> **J9** RCA |
| 11 | **LUMA** | OUT | Luminance + sync (~2 Vpp typ). S-video Y later if wanted |

### Where it sits on Retr01

```text
Color PROM DAC --RGB--> AD724 RIN/GIN/BIN
Beam PLDs ------sync--> HSYNC / VSYNC (or CSYNC)
Y3 FSC --------FIN----/
        |
     COMP --> J9 composite RCA
     RGB also still available on J2 directly from the DAC
```

## Sim notes

- Digital sims can stub ENCD/STND/SELECT/HSYNC/VSYNC and treat COMP as a logic "video present" flag.
- Full NTSC/PAL waveform fidelity is out of scope for a netlist logic sim.
- Pin names: `"RIN"`, `"COMP"`, `"HSYNC"`, `"FIN"`, ...
- Keep APOS/DPOS and AGND/DGND as separate supplies in the model even if the board ties them nearby.
