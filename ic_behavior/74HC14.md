# 74HC14 behavior

**74HC14** hex Schmitt-trigger inverter. **Optional** on Retr01. **Not** in the counted **19**.

**Package:** 14-pin **DIP**. **Family:** 74HC, 5 V on Retr01.

**Board:** `../general_docs/hardware.md` (skip conditions and when to populate).

---

## 1. Inputs (what pins can sense)

| Pin | Dir | What the net must provide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **nA** | IN | Logic level into inverter n (1..6). Schmitt inputs tolerate slow / noisy edges |
| **VCC** / **GND** | PWR | 5 V / GND |

Unused inputs must be tied (not floating).

### When Retr01 uses it

Skip on the first board when all of these hold:

- PHI2 and DOT come from **canned oscillators** (already square CMOS)
- Series **33 ohm** damping is enough on those clock nets
- Reset is a simple pull-up + switch (or supervisor) with no bounce/chatter on a scope

Populate (or leave a footprint) if bring-up shows soft clock edges, unavoidable crystal-buffer duty, or a noisy/slow reset rail that needs Schmitt cleanup.

### Stimulus checklist

| Stimulus | Expect |
| --- | --- |
| A rises through VT+ | Y falls (after hysteresis) |
| A falls through VT- | Y rises |
| Slow ramp on A | Clean single transition on Y (no chatter) |

---

## 2. Process (what happens inside)

Six independent inverters with input hysteresis. Y = !A with Schmitt thresholds (VT+ / VT-), not a single CMOS threshold.

Typical use on this board class: square PHI2/DOT from a soft source, or debounce/clean **RESET_N**.

No registers. Propagation delay is teens of ns @ 5 V.

---

| Pin | Dir | What the chip drives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **nY** | OUT | Inverted, cleaned logic level |

## Sim notes

- Model as INV with hysteresis thresholds, or a plain INV if the netlist never stresses slow edges.
- Pin names: `"1A"`, `"1Y"`, ...
- Only instantiate if the board BOM populates it.
