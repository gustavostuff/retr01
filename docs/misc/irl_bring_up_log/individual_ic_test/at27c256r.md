# AT27C256R color PROM

One kit chip in the socket at a time. The package is 600 mil, so it sits in a 28-pin socket of that width, one jumper per pin. The notch is pin 1. Power is off while the chip goes in or comes out. Pin 1 (VPP) ties to the same 5 V rail as pin 28 (VCC).

## Parts

| Part | Qty | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AT27C256R, PDIP-28, kit image | 2 | One chip in the socket at a time |
| 28-pin socket, 600 mil | 1 | Wide socket for the PROM |
| 100 nF | 1 | Bypass from VCC to ground |
| Switch | 6 | A5 through A0. One 6-position DIP switch covers the same pins |
| 10 kohm | 6 | Hold an open address switch at 0 |
| LED | 8 | One per data bit |
| 2.2 kohm | 8 | LED series resistor |

## Place

| What | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Pin 28 (VCC) and pin 1 (VPP) | +5 V |
| Pin 14 (GND) | Ground |
| 100 nF | +5 V rail to the ground rail, beside pin 28 |
| Pins 2, 3, 4, 21, 23, 24, 25, 26, 27 (A12, A7, A6, A10, A11, A9, A8, A13, A14) | Ground |
| Pin 20 (CE#) and pin 22 (OE#) | Ground |
| Pins 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10 (A5 through A0) | 10 kohm to ground, and a switch to +5 V |
| Pins 19, 18, 17, 16, 15, 13, 12, 11 (O7 through O0) | +5 V, then 2.2 kohm, then the LED anode. Cathode to the pin |

A high on A14 selects the upper 16 KB. Those bytes are `0xFF`. CE# or OE# high turns the data pins off. Pin 14 is ground, so the LED after pin 15 goes on pin 13. Closed is 1. A lit LED is a 0. A dark LED is a 1.

## Check

The switch code, A5 through A0, is the row in [`apps/common/r01_kit_palette.c`](../../../../apps/common/r01_kit_palette.c), starting at 0. Read the output byte from left to right on pins 19, 18, 17, 16, 15, 13, 12, 11. Pin 14 is ground, so it is skipped between 15 and 13.

| A5 to A0 | Row | O7 to O0 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 000000 | 0 | 00000000 |
| 000001 | 1 | 00100000 |
| 000100 | 4 | 00100100 |
| 001100 | 12 | 00000001 |
| 010000 | 16 | 00100101 |
| 100000 | 32 | 10010010 |
| 110000 | 48 | 11111111 |
| 111111 | 63 | 11110011 |

Power off, then the second chip goes in with the notch at the same end. The same switches light the same LEDs.
