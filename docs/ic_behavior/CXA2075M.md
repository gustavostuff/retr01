# CXA2075M behavior

Sony **CXA2075M** RGB encoder. Retr01 qty **1** on the motherboard (counted in the **19** motherboard ICs, **21** with cart).

**Package:** 24-pin plastic SOP (Sony SOP-24P-L01). Supply **5.0 V ± 0.25 V**. Generic parts (AliExpress and similar) are the supply. Sony no longer lists this device as a current catalog part.

Datasheet: [CXA2075M](https://www.alldatasheet.com/datasheet-pdf/pdf/146350/SONY/CXA2075M.html).

**Board:** `../general/hardware.md`. RGB analog comes from the color PROM DAC. Composite is **CVOUT** on J2 pin 4. The current KiCad landing at **U725** is still a 2x5 header. That header is not this pinout.

---

## 1. Inputs

| Pin | Name | What the pin takes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | **RIN** | Red. Black level is clamped. 100% is **1 Vp-p** maximum. Drive from a low impedance |
| 3 | **GIN** | Green. Same clamp and level |
| 4 | **BIN** | Blue. Same clamp and level |
| 6 | **SCIN** | Subcarrier. Sine or pulse, **0.4 to 5.0 Vp-p**. A pulse should stay near 50% duty |
| 7 | **NPIN** | NTSC when tied to VCC. PAL when tied to GND. Open sits near 1.7 V and is not a valid standard |
| 10 | **SYNCIN** | TTL composite sync. Low (at or below 0.8 V) is the sync period. High is at or above 2.0 V |

**VCC1** (pin 12) and **GND1** (pin 1) are the signal-side supply. **VCC2** (pin 19) and **GND2** (pin 24) are the output-driver supply. Each VCC pin gets its own bypass to its own ground, placed at the pin.

### Retr01 signals

| Signal | CXA2075M pin | Board |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Red, green, blue | RIN, GIN, BIN | Color PROM DAC |
| Subcarrier | SCIN | **Y3**. **3.579545 MHz** for NTSC, **4.433618 MHz** for PAL |
| Standard | NPIN | **J11**. Shunt 1-2 holds NPIN at +5 V (NTSC). Shunt 2-3 holds NPIN at GND (PAL) |
| Sync | SYNCIN | CSYNC from the beam PLDs. The same CSYNC is J2 pin 5 |
| Composite | CVOUT | J2 pin 4 |

HSYNC and VSYNC stay on the beam PLDs. They are not encoder inputs and they are not on J2. The raster stays **341 x 262** at about **60 Hz** for either standard. Y3 is the can that matches the J11 shunt.

## 2. Outputs

| Pin | Name | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 15 | **COUT** | Chroma. 75 ohm driver |
| 16 | **YOUT** | Luma. 75 ohm driver. YTRAP does not change this pin |
| 20 | **CVOUT** | Composite. 75 ohm driver |
| 21 | **BOUT** | Buffered blue. 75 ohm driver |
| 22 | **GOUT** | Buffered green. 75 ohm driver |
| 23 | **ROUT** | Buffered red. 75 ohm driver |

Pin 17 **YTRAP** is the trap for the subcarrier component in the composite luma path. When CVOUT is used, a capacitor, or a capacitor and an inductor in series, sits between YTRAP and GND. The value is chosen for the cross-color versus resolution tradeoff. The internal trap is the other option.

Pin 9 **BFOUT** is a burst-flag output and is unused on this board.

---

## 3. What this page does not lock

The SOP-24 pin numbers above are the Sony device. They are not the pin numbers of the 2x5 header in the current netlist. That header, the sim encoder stub, and the PCB footprint stay as they are until a later netlist pass.
