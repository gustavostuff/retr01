# MCP130 behavior

**Microchip MCP130** voltage supervisory circuit. Retr01 system reset manager.

**Package:** 3-pin **TO-92** (through-hole).

**Board:** `../general/hardware.md`. Controls CPU and system **RESB**.

---

## 1. Pins

| Pin | Name | Dir | What the net must provide |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **RESET#** | OD / OUT | Active-low reset output with internal 5k pull-up to VDD |
| 2 | **VDD** | PWR | +5 V supply |
| 3 | **VSS** | PWR | Ground (GND) |

A manual reset push-button connects between pin 1 (**RESET#**) and ground.

---

## 2. Process (what happens inside)

Monitors the 5 V rail on VDD:
- During power ramp-up, holds **RESET#** low until VDD crosses the threshold (4.60 V).
- Enforces an internal **350 ms** digital delay after VDD stabilizes above threshold before releasing **RESET#** high.
- If VDD drops below threshold (brown-out condition), pulls **RESET#** low immediately.
- When an external switch grounds **RESET#**, the output stays low while pressed. Upon button release, the 350 ms delay restarts before releasing **RESET#** high. This debounces mechanical contacts and eliminates chatter into the CPU **RESB** pin.

---

## 3. Package layout (TO-92)

```text
    +---------+
   /   MCP130  \   Flat face forward:
  |  1   2   3  |  Pin 1 = RESET#
  +----+---+----+  Pin 2 = VDD
       |   |   |   Pin 3 = VSS (GND)
```

| Num | Name | Dir | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | RESET# | OD | Connected to CPU RESB net and reset switch |
| 2 | VDD | PWR | Connected to +5 V rail |
| 3 | VSS | PWR | Connected to ground |
