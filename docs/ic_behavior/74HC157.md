# 74HC157 behavior

**74HC157** quad 2:1 multiplexer. Retr01 uses **three** DIP-16 parts for **VRAM A[11:0]** interleave (CPU vs beam).

**Package:** 16-pin **DIP**. **Family:** 74HC, 5 V on Retr01. Typical `tpd` ~11 ns @ 5 V.

**Board:** `../general/hardware.md`. Field SRAM uses MCU-S1 3-state on that path. VRAM A[11:0] is the muxed CPU vs beam set.

---

## 1. Inputs (what pins can sense)

| Pin | Dir | What the net must provide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A/B** | IN | Select. L = pass nA, H = pass nB |
| **nA, nB** | IN | Data inputs for channel n (1..4) |
| **G** | IN | Active-low strobe. When G is **high**, all Y are forced **low** (not Hi-Z) |
| **VCC** / **GND** | PWR | 5 V / GND |

### Retr01 wiring (logical)

| Port | Source |
| --- | --- |
| **A bus** | CPU VRAM address (PHI2 high / CPU phase) |
| **B bus** | Beam / BG-fetch address (PHI2 low) |
| **A/B, G** | PLD / PHI2 phase decode |

Each chip muxes **4** address bits. Three chips cover **A[11:0]**. Upper VRAM address bits come from PLD/glue as needed.

### Stimulus checklist

| Stimulus | Expect |
| --- | --- |
| G=L, A/B=L | Y copies A bus |
| G=L, A/B=H | Y copies B bus |
| G=H | All Y = 0 |

---

## 2. Process (what happens inside)

Four independent 2-line to 1-line selectors sharing one **A/B** select and one active-low **G**.

### Function

| G | A/B | Yn |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H | X | **L** (forced) |
| L | L | **nA** |
| L | H | **nB** |

Combinational only. No registers. Propagation delay is the whole story for sim timing.

### Why Retr01 needs three

VRAM AS6C62256 #2 is interleaved: CPU `$7F10-$7F12` on PHI2 high, BG fetch on PHI2 low. The mux picks which address reaches the SRAM without an MCU in the hard path.

---

## 3. Outputs (what pins can drive)

| Pin | Dir | What the chip drives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1Y-4Y** | OUT | Selected address bits toward VRAM A[] (forced L when G=H) |

### 16-pin DIP (sim pin table)

```text
         +-----\/-----+
    A/B  | 1       16 | VCC
     1A  | 2       15 | G
     1B  | 3       14 | 4A
     1Y  | 4       13 | 4B
     2A  | 5       12 | 4Y
     2B  | 6       11 | 3A
     2Y  | 7       10 | 3B
    GND  | 8        9 | 3Y
         +------------+
```

### Where outputs go

```text
CPU addr ----A----\
                   74HC157 --Y--> AS6C62256 #2 VRAM A[]
Beam addr ----B----/   ^
                       A/B, G from PLD / PHI2 phase
```

Register `"74HC157"` three times (`VRAM_MUX0`..`2` or similar).

## Sim notes

- Pure combinational eval from A/B/G and data inputs.
- Pin names: `"A/B"`, `"G"`, `"1A"`, `"1Y"`, ...
- G=H forces Y low, unlike a 245 which would Hi-Z.
