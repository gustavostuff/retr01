# 74HCU04 behavior

**74HCU04** hex unbuffered inverter. Retr01 crystal oscillator driver and clock buffer (qty **1** on motherboard).

**Package:** 14-pin **DIP**. **Family:** 74HC, 5 V on Retr01.

**Board:** `../general/hardware.md`. Drives Y1 (8.000 MHz) and Y2 (21.47727 MHz) crystal oscillator tanks.

---

## 1. Inputs and outputs

Contains six independent single-stage unbuffered CMOS inverters.

| Inverter | Role on Retr01 |
| --- | --- |
| Gate 1 | Y2 Pierce oscillator amplifier (21.47727 MHz crystal + 1M feedback resistor + 18 pF loads) |
| Gate 2 | Y2 buffer stage, feeding 21.47727 MHz square wave to 74HC74 clock divider |
| Gate 3 | Y1 Pierce oscillator amplifier (8.000 MHz crystal + 1M feedback resistor + 20 pF loads) |
| Gate 4 | Y1 buffer stage, feeding 8.000 MHz square wave to CPU PHI2 clock line |
| Gates 5-6 | Spare / tied inactive |

---

## 2. Process (what happens inside)

Unlike buffered inverters (74HC04), the **HCU** consists of a single CMOS complementary pair per gate without extra output buffer stages.

When biased with a 1 M ohm resistor between output and input, the gate operates in its linear amplification region. This provides high-bandwidth analog inverting gain with low phase delay, initiating fundamental-mode oscillation across the quartz crystal without spurious harmonic oscillation. The second inverter stage sharpens the resulting waveform into a full rail-to-rail digital square wave.

## 3. Package layout (14-pin DIP)

```text
         +-----\/-----+
     1A  | 1       14 | VCC
     1Y  | 2       13 | 6A
     2A  | 3       12 | 6Y
     2Y  | 4       11 | 5A
     3A  | 5       10 | 5Y
     3Y  | 6        9 | 4A
    GND  | 7        8 | 4Y
         +------------+
```

Pin 14 is VCC. Pin 7 is GND. Spare inputs 5A and 6A (pins 11 and 13) sit on net `U04_SPARE_IN`, strapped to GND in the U04 footprint. Spare outputs 5Y and 6Y (pins 10 and 12) are NC.
