# 74HC74 behavior

**74HC74** dual D-type positive-edge-triggered flip-flop. Retr01 dot clock divider (qty **1** on motherboard).

**Package:** 14-pin **DIP**. **Family:** 74HC, 5 V on Retr01.

**Board:** `../general/hardware.md`. Divides 21.47727 MHz master clock down to 5.369318 MHz.

---

## 1. Inputs and outputs

Contains two independent flip-flops with asynchronous set and reset.

| Flip-Flop | Role on Retr01 |
| --- | --- |
| Stage 1 | Divides 21.47727 MHz input by 2 down to 10.738636 MHz |
| Stage 2 | Divides 10.738636 MHz input by 2 down to 5.369318 MHz |

Both flip-flops are configured as toggle dividers by connecting the inverted output `/Q` back to the data input `D`.

---

## 2. Process (what happens inside)

Each rising clock edge on `CLK` latches the inverted state `/Q` into the flip-flop, toggling the output state:
- Stage 1 clocks on the 21.47727 MHz CMOS buffer output from the 74HCU04.
- Stage 2 clocks on the 10.738636 MHz output from Stage 1.
- Output from Stage 2 is the **5.369318 MHz** dot clock.

Because the output state changes once per incoming clock cycle, binary flip-flop division inherently enforces a symmetrical **50% duty cycle** square wave with sharp digital rise and fall times, feeding the video timing logic.

---

## 3. Package layout (14-pin DIP)

```text
         +-----\/-----+
    1CLR | 1       14 | VCC
      1D | 2       13 | 2CLR
    1CLK | 3       12 | 2D
    1PRE | 4       11 | 2CLK
      1Q | 5       10 | 2PRE
     1/Q | 6        9 | 2Q
     GND | 7        8 | 2/Q
         +------------+
```

Pin 14 is VCC. Pin 7 is GND. Pins 1, 4, 10, and 13 are PRE# and CLR# on net `U74_PRE_CLR#`, strapped to VCC in the U74 footprint so both flip-flops stay out of async set and reset. Those four pads are not VCC pins.
